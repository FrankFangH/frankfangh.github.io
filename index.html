Haohan (Frank) Fang

I completed my undergraduate studies at New York University, earning a joint B.A. in Mathematics and Computer Science with a minor in Game Design. At NYU's AI4CE Lab, I worked on multimodal proprioception for soft robotic grippers, combining visual and acoustic sensing to reconstruct finger shape and contact.

I am pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at Columbia University, where I focus on robotic perception, particularly multimodal sensing and geometry-aware learning for deformable robots. My goal is to develop robotic systems capable of estimating their own shape and physical interactions under occlusion.

Publication

IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) · 2026

Visual-Auditory Proprioception of Soft Finger Shape and Contact

Qinsong Guo*, Ke Yang*, Hanwen Zhao, Haohan Fang, Haoxuan Wang, Chen Feng

DeepCoFi is a lightweight multimodal proprioception framework that fuses internal camera images with acoustic spectrograms to jointly recover soft-finger geometry and contact. Its two-stage FoldingNet decoder first reconstructs global bending and then refines localized contact deformations.
